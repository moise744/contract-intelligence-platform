# Business Value Analysis

## Target Audience: Executives & Procurement

### 1. Cost Reduction (Operational Efficiency)
- **Problem**: Legal teams spend on average 3-4 hours manually reviewing a standard 50-page enterprise contract.
- **Solution**: The AI platform extracts key clauses and detects risks in under 30 seconds.
- **ROI**: Assuming 10,000 contracts a year, saving 3 hours per contract at $150/hr legal rate equals **$4.5M annual savings**.

### 2. Risk Mitigation
- **Problem**: Missing a termination penalty clause or a compliance failure (e.g., GDPR data handling) can cost millions in fines or lost revenue.
- **Solution**: The platform standardizes risk detection, flagging "High Risk" non-standard clauses immediately.
- **ROI**: Avoidance of catastrophic regulatory fines and ensuring SLA penalties are managed properly.

### 3. Accelerated Deal Velocity
- **Problem**: Procurement bottlenecks delay deal closures because contracts are stuck in the legal review queue.
- **Solution**: The Executive Summary allows business leaders to quickly understand the obligations without waiting for full legal breakdown.
- **ROI**: Faster time-to-revenue for sales contracts and faster vendor onboarding for procurement.

### 4. Obligation Management
- **Problem**: Companies often forget to cancel auto-renewing software contracts or miss deliverable deadlines.
- **Solution**: Obligation extraction automatically populates a calendar of deadlines.
- **ROI**: Eliminates zombie contract spending (typically 5-10% of total SaaS spend).
